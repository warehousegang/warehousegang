# Thomas Howard

**CTO · Engineer · Founder of BusyNow**

I'm CTO at Snap eHealth and the founder of BusyNow.

I work across AI-first engineering, platform engineering, Kubernetes, reliability, and the systems that allow small engineering teams to build and operate production software safely.

I'm particularly interested in a question:

> How much of an engineering organization can be encoded into software, agents, automation, policy, and platform infrastructure?

That's the idea behind what I call an **Engineering OS**.

## What I'm building

### BusyNow

A production application and my proving ground for Engineering OS.

I use BusyNow to explore Kubernetes, GitOps, platform APIs, reliability, AI-assisted engineering, and what it takes for a very small engineering organization to operate a real product.

[busynow.app](https://busynow.app/)

### YardMaster
[Yardmaster](https://github.com/warehousegang/yardmaster)

A Kubernetes-native project written in Go that expores controllers, custom resources, reconciliation loops, scheduling, and platform APIs.
